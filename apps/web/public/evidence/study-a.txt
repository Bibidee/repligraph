TITLE: Controlled caffeine reaction-time experiment
QUESTION: Does a 100 mg oral caffeine dose reduce mean simple visual reaction time in healthy adults aged 18 to 35 compared with placebo?
POPULATION: Healthy adults aged 18 to 35
DESIGN: Randomized double-blind placebo-controlled experiment
INTERVENTION: 100 mg oral caffeine after 12-hour caffeine abstinence
COMPARATOR: Placebo
OUTCOME: Mean simple visual reaction time in milliseconds
TIMING: 45 minutes after dosing, computerized 50-trial task
CONCLUSION: The caffeine group showed a lower mean reaction time than placebo.
